## Test automation optimization program

Test automation should provide valuable feedback, fast, but it will only do so when the tests are designed, organized and maintained in a way that fits your application, architecture and development process. As test suites grow, it’s easy to accumulate slow, brittle or redundant tests that consume increasing amounts of time without providing the value you're looking for.

This tailored engagement helps your organisation identify where your test automation is holding you back, determine where the biggest opportunities for improvement lie, and optimize your approach to give your teams valuable feedback, fast, with expert guidance and hands-on support every step of the way.

## Contract testing adoption accelerator

Implementing contract testing can significantly improve confidence in distributed systems, but only when you go in with the right expectations, and when the chosen workflow fits your development process and team dynamics.

This tailored engagement helps your organisation determine whether contract testing is the right fit for your integration testing challenges, and when it is, to design an effective adoption strategy and implement it successfully, with expert guidance every step of the way.
