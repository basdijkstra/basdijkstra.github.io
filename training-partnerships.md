## An experienced specialist to complement your training portfolio

I've been working in software testing and test automation since 2006. Over the years, I've designed and delivered customized technical training programmes for engineering teams around the world.

My focus is on practical, hands-on training that helps participants develop skills they can apply directly in their day-to-day work. Rather than delivering generic, off-the-shelf courses, I work with organizations to tailor the content, exercises and examples to their technologies, challenges and experience levels.

Areas I regularly cover in my courses and workshops include:

- **Test automation engineering:** designing, building and maintaining effective automated testing solutions.
- **Test automation strategy and architecture:** helping engineering teams make better decisions about their automation approach and technical foundations.
- **Contract testing:** introducing and accelerating the adoption of contract testing in distributed systems.
- **AI-assisted testing and automation:** exploring how AI can help engineering teams build and maintain more effective automated tests.

My training programmes are designed for software engineers, test automation engineers, testers and engineering teams who want to improve their technical capabilities.

## Training programmes that go beyond individual training days

I particularly enjoy working on customized, multi-day programmes for engineering organizations. Depending on the subject and the customer's needs, an engagement might involve:

- A tailored, multi-day technical training programme
- An intensive practical workshop addressing a specific engineering challenge
- A training and adoption accelerator combining knowledge transfer with hands-on implementation
- A series of workshops helping multiple teams adopt new practices and technologies

This makes it possible to offer your customers more than a standard training course: a focused programme designed around their specific situation and intended outcomes.

I can also work with you to develop new training offerings that complement your existing portfolio.

## How we can collaborate

I'm flexible in how a collaboration is organized. Possibilities include:

* **Co-branded training** - we jointly offer a training programme, combining your customer relationships and organizational capabilities with my technical expertise and delivery experience.
* **Direct customer engagements** - you introduce me to an organization that needs specialized training or consulting. We can then agree on the most appropriate commercial and delivery arrangement.
* **White-label delivery** - you sell and organize the training under your own brand, while I take care of the technical preparation and delivery.

I'm open to discussing other arrangements as well. My goal is to establish a collaboration that works commercially and professionally for both parties.

## International delivery

I'm based in the Netherlands and regularly deliver training internationally, both on-site and online.

I particularly enjoy working with engineering teams in different countries and adapting training programmes to their working environments, technologies and challenges.

For on-site engagements, I'm available to travel internationally. In fact, [I prefer running my courses and workshops on-site](/why-i-prefer-on-site-over-online-training/). However, online delivery is also possible when it makes sense for the programme and participants.
