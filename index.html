We test because we want to be informed about the state of the product we're working on, and about any potential problems that might occur when people use our product.

We use test automation to get valuable feedback, fast. But achieving that requires more than choosing the right tools and writing more tests. It requires a thoughtful approach to what we test, how we test it, and how we use the feedback to make better decisions.

That's where I come in.

With 20 years of experience in software testing and test automation, I've helped teams and organisations around the world improve their testing practices, solve technical challenges, and get more value from their automated tests.

I work with teams through hands-on training, consulting and mentoring, helping them tackle real problems in their own context and build the skills to keep improving long after our engagement ends. Whether we're developing a test automation strategy, improving an existing test suite, or exploring new approaches, the focus is always on practical, lasting results.

Want to find out how I can help your team get more valuable feedback, faster? Let's talk.
